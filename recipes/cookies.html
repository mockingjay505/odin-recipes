Chocolate Chip Cookies

Description

Whether you like your chocolate chip cookies crispy or chewy, these sweet treats are the definition of cozy comfort food. They’re the best of both worlds, soft in the center and crispy on the edges. They’re not difficult to make either — our simple chocolate chip cookie recipe doesn’t require any special equipment, so it’s easy to whip up any night of the week and is a great project to tackle with kids. Our experts specifically designed this recipe to be easy to prep in advance, too. Simply mix the ingredients as outlined in the recipe, then portion out the dough and freeze.
